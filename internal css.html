Artificial Intelligence

What is AI?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. AI can learn from data, understand language, recognize images, solve problems, and make decisions.

Core Pillars of AI

Machine Learning (ML): A subset of AI where computers learn from data without being explicitly programmed for every single rule. Instead, algorithms find patterns in massive datasets to make predictions or decisions.

Deep Learning: An advanced branch of machine learning inspired by the human brain's neural networks. It uses multi-layered neural networks (hence "deep") to process complex data like images, audio, and natural language.

Natural Language Processing (NLP): The technology that allows machines to understand, interpret, and generate human language (both text and speech), powering tools like chatbots, translators, and voice assistants.

Computer Vision: Enables computers to "see" and interpret visual information from the world, used in facial recognition, medical imaging, and autonomous vehicles.
